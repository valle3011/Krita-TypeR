Stroke centre lines in strokelines.txt
=====================================

The file holds no glyph outlines. It is a set of polylines through the
middle of each stroke of a letter, in the order and the direction the
stroke is written, built by tools/make_strokes.py from two sources.

The lines for the kana, for the iteration mark and for the Japanese comma,
full stop and exclamation mark are derived from KanjiVG's stroke paths:
scaled and simplified, but the strokes and their order are KanjiVG's work.

Simplex Roman, fitted onto the same frame as the kana.


Drawn here
----------

Neither source has the corner brackets, the wave dash, the ellipsis or the
eszett, and neither carries the two marks over a German vowel. Those are a
handful of straight lines and ticks written out in tools/make_strokes.py.
